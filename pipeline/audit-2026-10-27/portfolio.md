# Audit: Joseph Bell portfolio site

**Verdict:** Needs work · **Readiness:** 4/10

Not ready to send. The build is clean, but three things block it: the share link unfurls as "Claude Artifact", which breaks the no-AI rule; the "hand-drawn / I illustrate it myself" headline is false because draw.py generated every drawing; and on phones the AI-check page's "How the check works" is blank. It also shows none of Joseph's real website work, has a "Portrait to come [TO CONFIRM]" box in About, and misstates the blind-test scoring and who marked it.

## Strengths to keep

- Technically clean: no console errors or failed requests at 390, 768, 844 landscape or 1280. Both web fonts load, with metric-matched fallbacks. The page is light (~174KB) with no layout shift, and the scroll signature doesn't scroll-jack.
- The no-JS and reduced-motion states are a properly designed static before/after, not a blank stage.
- Contact details are correct everywhere (Joseph Bell, 07931 373488 / tel:+447931373488, jbell5785@gmail.com). There are no business names, no fake clients or testimonials, and no mention of AI in how the work is made; the only leak is the share link.
- The swap-test idea is a clear, memorable way to explain the method. Step 1's mark-up ('stock photo: could be anywhere', 'old PDF = old prices') will land with owners.
- The AI check is framed honestly: 'Accuracy, not rankings. Nobody can promise rankings', with a full caveats list. Most items in the Found list are concrete and checkable.
- The material for a strong portfolio already exists: the 12 concepts in the repo (Tide Tables A, Matsuba B, Caffè Mamma C, checked by screenshot) are distinctive and far more impressive than what the portfolio currently shows.

## Verified findings (ranked)

### [BLOCKER · medium] The share link reveals the tooling: it unfurls as 'Claude Artifact' with Claude's logo, it's private, and there's no domain

*Lens:* function+content+design (B2, B4, D05, m18) · *Verifier:* confirmed

**Evidence.** curl https://claude.ai/artifact/DbXDkuyiuLfz9QJb1LsjCN returns <title>Claude Artifact</title>, og:title 'Claude Artifact', og:description 'Try out Artifacts created by Claude users' and og:image claude_ogimage.png. The page has no canonical, og:url or og:image. EMAILS.md itself warns that claude.ai links 'show how the work was made' and that call and email buttons may do nothing inside the viewer; Email and Call are the site's only calls to action.

**Fix.** Buy the domain and deploy the folder (minus NOTES.md and _drawings/) to Cloudflare Pages or Netlify. Add canonical, og:url, a 1200x630 og:image, absolute links in llms.txt and a sitemap line in robots.txt. Never put a claude.ai link in an outreach email.

Remove hand-/pen/notebook/'I drew'/'illustrate it myself' from the H1, title, meta, alt text, figcaption and About, and keep 'All drawings are original'. While rewriting the H1, add the owner's outcome (e.g. 'Websites that look like your place and get your facts right'). Have Joseph confirm every first-person method line.

## Refuted by the verifier

- **'I go and look before I draw anything' may be untrue**: There's no evidence he didn't visit. Joseph lives in Richmond, worked at one of the places, and the Tide Tables email calls him a regular. Photos used as references don't rule out a visit. The 'draw' half is covered by V2, whose fix asks Joseph to confirm every first-person method line.
- **The redesign was written from the same facts used for marking**: That's the mechanism being tested (facts on the page get repeated), not circularity. It doesn't harm the pitch, and V7 covers the wording.
- **Gable 'over the door' and 'oak' slats**: It can't be shown wrong (the central TEAS gable may sit above the door), and it's trivial to any owner.
- **The Gmail address weakens credibility**: The brief mandates these contact details. A domain mailbox is optional once V1 is done.
- **No price indication**: Leaving prices out is a deliberate decision in NOTES and HANDOVER. The missing care plan and inclusions are kept as V14.
- **The desktop signature stage ignores the notebook grid**: In the 1280 screenshots the mock and steps are centred as one unit and look deliberate. No harm.
- **The 'one highlighter per screen' rule is broken**: That's an internal design rule owners never see. No effect on the pitch.
- **Too many button, link and rule styles**: They're consistent with the notebook system on screen. Subjective, with no visible harm.
- **A fast flick shows half-drawn frames**: Those are normal mid-transition frames. Animations complete, so it isn't a defect.
- **The tape strips don't feel physical**: Subjective, and it doesn't affect credibility.
- **Dead space under the hero at 1920**: Negligible, and owners review on phones.
- **The desktop 'Call' button opens a tel: handler**: That's standard practice, and the number is printed in the hero and contact rows.
- **Notch safe areas on the hosted build**: Not tested on a device, specific to the claude.ai host, and moot once V1 is done.
- **Print and PDF output is poor**: Owners won't print a portfolio. No effect on the pitch.
- **The 'eyes drop' moment is imperceptible**: Owners are never told to expect it. No effect on the pitch.
- **Case arrows animate off-screen at load**: Invisible to visitors and harmless.
- **Person.email uses a 'mailto:' prefix**: schema.org's own Person examples use mailto:. It's valid.
