La storia

The restaurant with the washing

Caffè Mamma opened on Hill Street in Richmond, serving pasta. The room was dressed like a street in Naples, with washing lines strung overhead and murals on the walls, and locals soon had a name for it: the restaurant with the washing.

1983to confirm Hill Street, Richmond

Then came a move to Rocks Lane in Barnes, as Mamma Ristorante, and a longer menu: meat, fish and more.

2004 Rocks Lane, Barnes

In March 2006 it came back to Richmond, its spiritual home, and opened here at 110 Kew Road.

2006 110 Kew Road

The line is still up. It runs across the dining room under a deep-blue ceiling, hung now with sports shirts. Below it: white cloths, yellow napkins, a yellow rose on every table, and a curved staircase up to the function room.

An Italian kitchen with a Portuguese family behind it, and family-run to this day.to confirm
