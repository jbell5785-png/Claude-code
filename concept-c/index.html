La storiathe story

First it was the restaurant with the washing. It still is.

1983, Hill Street.to confirm Caffè Mamma opened in Richmond serving pasta, in a room dressed like a street in Naples: washing lines strung overhead, murals on the walls. Locals had a name for it: the restaurant with the washing.

2004, Barnes. A move to Rocks Lane as Mamma Ristorante, and main courses joined the pasta: meat, fish and more.

March 2006, Kew Road. Back to Richmond, its spiritual home, at number 110.

Today. The line still runs across the dining room, hung with sports shirts now, under a deep-blue ceiling. White cloths, yellow napkins, a yellow rose on every table.

Family-run: an Italian kitchen with a Portuguese family behind it.to confirm
