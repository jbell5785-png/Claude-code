## 2. Hours

### Opening hours
- **Shop and tearoom:** "Mon - Fri, 9am - 6pm / Weekends, 9am - 6pm" ([S1]). Content pages say "Weekdays 9am – 6:00pm / Weekends 9am – 6:00pm" ([S2], [S3], [S13]).
- No separate shop and tearoom hours are published. TO CONFIRM.

### Service times
- **Breakfast:** "Last Orders at 11am for Cooked or Toasted Items" ([S6] menu). "On Thursdays, we do not serve cooked breakfasts, but bakery pastries remain fully available." ([S6])
- **Luncheon:** "Monday - Sunday 12:00 - 15:00" ([S7]).
- **Set teas:** "Set Tea Sittings All Day Monday - Sunday". High Tea, Savoury High Tea and The Special Taster Set Tea from 10:00; Prosecco and Champagne High Tea from 11:00 ([S8]). The page's meta description says "Afternoon Teas can be booked from 10:00am till 17:00".
- **Walk-ins:** "Tables are available throughout the day on a first-come, first-served basis"; "Last walk-ins must be seated by 17:30" ([S8]).

### Deliveries and shipping
- **Local delivery:** within a 1.5-mile radius, Monday to Thursday ([S1]).
- **Postal shipping:** "We ship Monday through to Thursday for deliveries on Tuesday, Wednesday Thursday. We are currently unable to send via postal courier at the weekend." Couriers are DPD and DHL ([S12]).
- **Free shipping:** on orders over £100 ([S1]).
- **Collection:** orders can be placed online for collection ([S1]). Deliveroo is also mentioned ([S1]).

### National Archives café
- Public hours on [S11]: "09:00 and 17:00 on Tuesday and Thursday and between 09:00 and 16:00 on Wednesday, Friday and Saturday". Sunday opening is selective.
- **Stale:** the page lists Christmas 2023 closures.

## 4. Website audit (fair)

### Mobile at 390px
- **Overflow:** No page-level horizontal overflow on any of the 20 Shopify pages loaded. scrollWidth was 390 everywhere. The slideshow track runs off-screen but is clipped, as expected for a carousel.
- **Guest rooms site:** maidshotel.co.uk (Wix) measured scrollWidth 986 at 390. Because the viewport was resized rather than reloaded, Wix may not have re-laid out the page. TO CONFIRM on a device.
- **First screen:**
  - A centred logo with its drawing, a hamburger menu, search and cart.
  - A black announcement bar ("LOCAL DELIVERY IS BACK DIRECT FROM MAIDS…", rotating with "FREE SHIPPING On all orders over £100").
  - A hero slideshow; it showed the tart slide, "THE HISTORIC / MAIDS OF HONOUR TART / A Royal Secret....".
  - **No booking button, hours or phone number on the first screen.** The hero's top and bottom text lines were hard to read over the photo while they animated in.
- **Tap targets under 44px:**
  - Header menu, search and cart: 34×45.
  - Slide buttons ("SEE THE FULL IMAGE" 203×34, "SHOP NOW" 127×38, "BOOK A TABLE" 156×38).
  - Breadcrumb "Home": 35×20.
  - "Email (click me)": 105×22.
  - Footer "BOOK HERE" buttons are small outlined boxes.
- **Hours on mobile:** The homepage hours block sits about 3,800px down, roughly 4.5 screens. On desktop, a "Contact & Opening Hours" link sits in the top utility bar. That bar was not visible on the mobile first screen.
- **Menus on mobile:** Every food and drink menu is an A4 JPEG (2479×3508) with `alt=""`, shrunk to about 350px wide. The text cannot be read without zooming, cannot be searched, and screen readers get nothing.
- **Booking iframe:** In the mobile capture of /pages/set-tea-booking, the area where the ResDiary iframe sits was blank. This may be a capture-timing artefact. TO CONFIRM on a device.
- **Lazy loading:** In the full-page captures, the collection tiles and blog tiles appeared as grey placeholders. That is lazy loading in the capture, not necessarily a fault on the site.

### Carousels and slideshows
- **Hero:** Impulse slideshow, autoplay at 8 seconds (`data-speed="8000"`), with arrows, dots and progress bars. There is a "Pause slideshow" control, which is good, but it measured 1×1, which suggests it is visually hidden or very small. Five slides:
  1. "THE ORIGINAL STAFF IN THE 1890S";
  2. "THE HISTORIC MAIDS OF HONOUR TART / A Royal Secret....";
  3. "SHIPPING NATIONWIDE AND ON DERLIVEROO / NEWENS BAKERS BREAD CART";
  4. "SERVING YOU SINCE 1850 / Shop & Tea Room on 288 Kew Road";
  5. "BOOK A TABLE | VIST THE SHOP".
- **Announcement bar:** rotates two messages, with a 5-second autoplay setting present.
- **Below the hero:** the homepage is long. It holds about 14 more sections (collections grid, booking, address, "A Slice of History" blog grid, tiles, guest rooms, films and photoshoots, vegan options, personalised pies, wedding cakes, special occasion cakes, booking footers).

### How hard it is to find hours, menus and booking
- **Hours:** one link on the desktop top bar; buried on mobile; repeated at the bottom of every content page. Service times (11am breakfast cut-off, 12:00–15:00 luncheon, 17:30 last walk-in, no cooked breakfast on Thursday) appear **only** on the booking pages.
- **Menus:** under "Our Menus", where each menu is combined with its booking page ("Breakfast Menu & Booking", and so on). Menus are images only.
- **Booking:** there are four different ResDiary widgets. The homepage uses a "ThreeMonth" widget and each meal page uses a "Standard" one, so it is not clear which to use for what.

### Other issues
- Almost every homepage image has `alt=""`, including the 1890s photo and the frontage.
- The National Archives page lists "Christmas 2023" closures and old events.
- The opening hours do not separate the shop from the tearoom.
- The luncheon minimum spend is £20 on the page and £22 on the menu.

### Strengths to keep
- **A real archive:**
  - the 1890s A.N. Newens photo;
  - the Newens Bakers tricycle and cart;
  - the 1907 Hill Street postcard;
  - mid-century interiors;
  - the post-war frontage;
  - the 1924 poster.
- **The logo drawing and blackletter wordmark**, which are distinctive and already line art.
- **Long, warm history writing**, including the Blitz story and the 1947 surveyor quote.
- **Complete menus with prices**, plus a clear allergen statement on the Patisserie List.
- **Booking split by meal**, all on one booking system (ResDiary).
- **Detailed shipping and packaging information**, a working shop with collection, postal and local delivery, and gift vouchers.
- **A pause control on the slideshow**, and no page-level horizontal overflow at 390px.

## 5. Unknowns
- **The founding date at Kew Road** (1850, 1860, 1868 or 1887), and whether the business is still family-run.
- **Separate shop and tearoom hours**, bank holiday and Christmas hours, and whether the Thursday no-cooked-breakfast rule is current.
- **The Luncheon minimum spend** (£20 or £22). Are the 2024 menu images current?
- **Ownership and listing:** whether 288 Kew Road is listed or locally listed. No listing was found; Historic England's "Maids of Honour Tea Shop" listing refers to Hill Street ([S24b]).
- **Date and meaning of the photos:** the exact date of the A.N. Newens photo, and what the coat of arms above that fascia means.
- **Walking time and nearest gate** from the shop to a Kew Gardens gate.
- **The china pattern** (it looks like Spode "Blue Italian").
- **The source of the Cadbury's sign** in the red room.
- **Vector logo and photo rights:** whether a vector logo exists, and whether the archive photos can be used.
- **Mobile checks on a device:** the guest rooms site overflow, and the ResDiary iframe on mobile.
- **The tart's filling:** the business does not disclose it.
- **Wheelchair access:** the business site does not state its accessibility. The National Archives café is "Fully Accessible" ([S11]).
