Legend

Part of Richmond’s history for nearly 500 years….

“It all started when Henry VIII, King of England from 1509 to 1547 came across Anne Boleyn and her maids of honour, eating the dainty tarts from a silver dish. Tasting one, the King was so delighted by its ‘melt-in-the-mouth’ sensation that he confiscated the recipe and demanded that it be kept secret in a locked iron box at Richmond Palace. … the Maids of Honour tart recipe remains the best kept secret in town!”

From the boxed text on the High Tea menu. The story is a legend.

Please be aware that due to the open plan nature of our kitchens we are unable to guarantee a complete absence of gluten, however, we do offer dishes and products that are made with non-gluten containing ingredients. In our Craft Bakery products are made where nuts, gluten, sesame seeds, milk, eggs, mustard and soya are used, so may contain traces. Some of our products may also contain traces of peanut due to ingredients from our suppliers.

Online orders

Whilst we aim to provide relevant information on our website (or in store) concerning allergens in our products, if you do have an allergy please inform staff or add a note to your online order. This allows us to check that both the product selected is allergen free and so enable us to minimise any cross contamination of trace of allergen within your order. Maids of Honour is unable to guarantee against a trace of any allergen as we cook in an open air kitchen and we do use certain allergens in many of our products.

Vegan options

We are frequently working on new vegan recipes for our savoury and sweet counters. If you, or any of your party are vegan, please call and speak to the team and we will be more than happy to discuss all of our vegan options currently available. For any bookings or take away orders we need a minimum of 24 hours notice period. 020 8940 2752

Service

Service is not included in menu prices. A discretionary service charge of 12.5% will be added to bill. All gratuities will be shared with the staff of the day.
