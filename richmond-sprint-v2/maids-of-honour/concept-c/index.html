The building

Still here.

A Victorian bakery’s shop, bombed in the war, rebuilt by the family in Mock Tudor, and baking again on the same spot on the Kew Road. Three plates for the rail.

The Victorian shop 1947 Today

The bakery

Warm from our ovens.

“Everything at the Original Maids of Honour is produced ‘in house’. We do not ‘buy in’ and ‘sell on’.” Pies, pasties, quiches, sausage rolls, scones and cakes, made “every day, seven days a week”.

Our Famous Maids of Honour Tart

“Deliciously sweet and yet slightly savoury, light and crisp yet unctuously soft in the middle.” £3.50 at the table, £2.25 from the shop. Contains milk, wheat, gluten and eggs.

Legend Legend has it that Henry VIII kept the recipe under lock and key at Richmond Palace. And the recipe today? “That’s still a secret – one that’s safe with us for a good long time to come…”
