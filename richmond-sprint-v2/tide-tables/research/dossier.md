# Tide Tables Café: research dossier (v2)

All sources were accessed on **25 October 2026**.

**Labels:**
- **VERIFIED** means an authority, the café's own site, or two independent sources agree.
- **REPORTED** means a single source, possibly dated.
- **UNKNOWN** means no usable source was found.

**Method:**
- Pages were loaded with default Playwright Chromium or WebFetch, at most twice per page.
- Refusals are recorded in § 8 and were not worked around.
- No review quotes are used and no staff are named.

## 4. Practical facts

- **Menu:**
  - **No current menu is published anywhere official.** The homepage says: "You can now view our menu here." The word "here" is **not a link**, and no menu file or page exists on the site (S1).
  - The next line reads: "Please note that this is a sample menu, and all items and prices are subject to change…"
  - Only a "taster" list is published (S1): Popina tarts; vegetable lasagne; jacket potatoes; pies; vegetable and bean chilli with brown rice; hearty salads; herbal teas; Fairtrade gourmet coffee; freshly squeezed organic juices and smoothies; organic milk; wine and beer.
  - **No prices are published officially.**
  - Third-party price: vegetarian lasagne and salad for £10 (S7, **2017**, not usable as current).
  - The chalkboard in the third-party vault photo (S24-1, ~5 months old) shows these headings: "Welcome to Tide Tables Café", "Coffee", "…& Tea", "Organic Wines & Beers", "All our wines are vegan" (partly legible). Partly legible drinks lines: red / white / rosé wine, lager, organic cider, organic ale, prosecco, and an ID notice for anyone who appears under 21. **Prices are illegible.** The board is not usable as a menu.
- **Booking / walk-in:** No booking system or policy is published. The site warns "we get very crowded on sunny days" (S1). Parties are arranged by phone on 07922474327 (S1). Walk-in-only status: UNKNOWN [TO CONFIRM].
- **Accessibility:**
  - VisitRichmond: "Disabled access" (S16, REPORTED).
  - The photos show a level paved approach to the glazed entrance, with a drainage channel at the threshold (S24-3). A short flight of steps is visible at the garden-side opening (S2 `thumbs_img_3476-2`).
  - The garden is gravel (S24-4).
  - Step-free route, accessible toilet and baby change: UNKNOWN.
- **Dogs:** No policy published. A dog appears among the garden tables in the café's own 2018 photo (S2 `thumbs_img_6870`). Policy UNKNOWN [TO CONFIRM].
- **Wi-Fi:** "Free wireless internet… free WiFi in our cafe" (S1). A 2018 chalkboard reads "FREE WiFi" (S1 `column-3.jpg`).
- **Payment:** Credit cards accepted, no fee (S16, REPORTED). Cash: UNKNOWN.
- **Hire and events:**
  - Party hosting at either café, with indoor and outdoor space and full catering (S1).
  - Upcoming events: none listed ("We are seeking interesting local events to host on our homepage for the next three months…").
  - The Jam Jar music nights are "starting again soon" (stale).
  - Capacity, pricing and terms: UNKNOWN.
- **Hollyhock Café:**
  - Terrace Gardens on Richmond Hill, "nestled in a quaint garden setting with river views" (S1).
  - Same management as Tide Tables (S1, S7).
  - A rustic building in a high position with a veranda (S7, 2017). A thatched tea-house at the centre of the gardens (S15, REPORTED).
  - Open 7 days during daylight hours, with gender-neutral toilets (S14).
  - A wood-burning stove and a terrace are mentioned in a Spotted by Locals summary (page refused, so UNVERIFIED).
  - Walk from Tide Tables: upstream along the towpath to Grotto Gate, then a tunnel and steps up (S7). Distance UNKNOWN.

## 5. Website audit (tidetablescafe.com, loaded 25 Oct 2026)

**Stack:**
- WordPress 6.9.10 (generator meta).
- Theme: **"VW Interior Designs"**, an interior-design theme (stylesheet path `/themes/vw-interior-designs/`).
- Six pages: Home, Gallery, Press, Contact, Feedback, Subscribe.

### Mobile, 390 × 844
- **No horizontal overflow** on any page: scrollWidth equals clientWidth at 390 for all six pages.
- **First screen** (top to bottom):
  - a green "Menu" bar with a hamburger (390 × 47 px tap target, fine);
  - the site title "Tide Tables Cafe" in widely letter-spaced white Titillium over a tart close-up, with the strapline "Richmond Riverside Cafe";
  - a **full-width search box**;
  - the H1 "**Home**", then H3 "Tide Tables Cafe" and the 276 × 106 "FOOD" thumbnail.
  - **The first screen shows no arch, river, address, hours or phone number.**
- The nav button is labelled **"Menu"**, which visitors may read as the food menu, and there is no food menu.
- **Phone numbers and email are plain text**: no `tel:` or `mailto:` links on any page, so nothing is tap-to-call. There is no map or map link.
- Body copy is justified, which causes word gaps at 390 px (for example "For information email hey@… or call").
- The homepage is about 4,430 px tall on mobile, in a single column of text.
- Desktop nav links are 48 px tall, fine.

### Stale or broken content (quoted)
- Home: "**Spring is here** and if you've been away for the winter months, we'd love to invite you back…". This is also the meta description. It was seen in October, and the page was last modified 27 Feb 2026.
- Home: "**Live music nights will be starting again soon… Please watch this space for more information.**"
- Home: "Upcoming Richmond Events… We are seeking interesting local events to host on our homepage for the next three months…". No events are listed.
- Home: "**You can now view our menu here.**" The word "here" has no link.
- Footer on all pages: "**Copyright 2018**".
- Press:
  - "Looking forward to Richmond … May Fair and Chestnut Sunday" and "Are you joining The Richmond Riverside Walk on the 19th May…". Undated.
  - Links to an old `visitrichmond.co.uk/thedms.asp?...` URL and an old charity event URL.
  - Also contains a customer testimonial (not reproduced here).
- **Press page corruption:** the token "**Omisga**" is inserted mid-sentence several times, e.g. "The summer season is Omisga almost upon us", "We have a menu Omisga full of healthy options". It is also in the page title "**Press - Tide Tables Cafe Omisga**". The page was modified 28 Feb 2026. **This looks like injected or corrupted text. Recommend the client check the site's integrity.**
- Subscribe:
  - Generic boilerplate ("we also tweet fascinating facts… about nature", "preview our latest tweets directly from the sidebar"). **There is no sidebar, no social links and no sign-up form.**
  - The page was modified 28 Feb 2026.
- Feedback: "Your feedback, once moderated for safe content, will appear on the 'News' page…". **There is no News page.**
- Typos:
  - "Thinking of hosting a **part**?"
  - "the best place in **Richmodn**"
  - "Stop in **a** ask for a free local map"
  - "**Surounding** nature"
  - "shopping, **dinning**"
