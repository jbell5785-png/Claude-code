Come in
  1. Under the carved face and in. The whole café is one long, whitewashed brick vault.
  2. Ferns in hanging baskets. Strings of lights along the curve.
  3. Grey polka-dot oilcloth, mismatched wooden chairs, and the long counter with its chalkboards.
  4. And at the far end, a door: out to the garden, the plane trees and the Thames.

Out the far door

You’ll find people out in the garden all year round.

We have a large garden with three majestic plane trees. The views of the Thames are some of the best in London.

Watch out though, we get very crowded on sunny days.

We are also just by the embarkation point for boats to Hampton Court, and for boats that head to the city during the summer months. to confirm

Getting here

Richmond is easily accessible by London Underground, Overground and mainline trains as well as a huge number of buses. Once you reach Richmond, head straight for Richmond Bridge, only a few minutes away, and Tide Tables is in the arch beneath.

Because we are right on the Thames Path, lots of people take a break from their walk and stop in for refreshments.
