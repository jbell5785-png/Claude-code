The Thames at Richmond is tidal.

This page's drawn tideMid-tide

Downstream, Richmond's half-tide weir, opened in 1894, raises its gates for about two hours either side of high water, and the rest of the time holds the river here at or above half-tide.

Despite the name, there are no tide times on this site. The tide you scroll through is drawn, not measured.

To the sister café

The walk to Hollyhock

“Looking for Hollyhock? Stop in and ask for a free local map!”

Hollyhock Café is up in Terrace Gardens. This is only our sketch of the walk, drawn from the café's directions. Ask for the real map when you stop in.

  1. Start under the bridge

    Tide Tables, in a converted arch beneath Richmond Bridge, on the Thames Path.

  2. Follow the towpath upstream

    From Richmond Bridge, follow the towpath upstream, past the houses and gardens. The river stays on your right.

  3. Grotto Gate, low on the left

    Look for Grotto Gate low on the left, before Petersham Meadows. If you reach the meadows, you've gone too far.

  4. Through the tunnel, up the steps

    Take the tunnel and the steps up into Terrace Gardens.

  5. Hollyhock Café

    “Situated in the Terrace Gardens with a splendid view over the gardens and the River Thames.” Open during daylight hours.
