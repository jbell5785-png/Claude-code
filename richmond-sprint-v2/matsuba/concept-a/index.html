Skip the walk to booking

Step inside

The long room

From the bar to the far slat wall, where you book. Swipe the cards or tap a stop.

Just inside the door

The bar

Glasses hang upturned beneath a perforated metal canopy, with a shelf of sake along the top.

Down one side

The brick wall

Black swing-arm lamps reach out over the tables, with fairy lights strung in swags between them. The tables run in single file along the banquette.

Down the other

The booths

Booths behind perforated screens, white marble-look tables and black pebble chopstick rests.

At the far end

The slat wall

Oak slats in two layers, the front one stepping down a slat at a time. This is as far as the room goes, and where you book.

Find the corner

Red Lion Street “was a narrow and winding alley”, widened in the early 20th century. No. 10 is part of “the 1920s terrace of shops at nos. 8-32 (even)”.

Source: Richmond Council, Central Richmond Conservation Area Appraisal (2023).
