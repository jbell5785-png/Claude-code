The room

Two layers of oak, stepping down one slat at a time.

Down the side of the long room and across the far end, square oak slats rise from a grey wall in two layers. The back layer runs up to the ceiling. The front layer stops short, and its tops step down one slat at a time in a straight line. Brass twin sconces throw two warm glows across the wood.

This proposal is built on that wall. The step on the wall behind this page moves down as you read. On the menu, each course is a slat. Every light you switch on here is one of those sconces.
