# Answers: Matsuba, Richmond (evidence pack Y)

## 1. Where exactly is Matsuba in Richmond, and what's its phone number?
Matsuba is at 10 Red Lion Street, Richmond TW9 1RW. The restaurant's own site says to look for the two slate awnings on the corner. Harden's places it on the fringe of the town centre, near St Mary Magdalene. The phone number is 020 8605 3513, and the email is info@matsuba.co.uk.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (homepage, footer, JSON-LD); https://matsuba.co.uk/llms.txt; Harden's Matsuba page; Foodle; Wanderlog; Trip.com.
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** Harden's lists a different website and email (matsuba-restaurant.com), and that domain does not resolve. I used the official matsuba.co.uk details, which Foodle and Wanderlog also point to. The address and phone number agree across all sources.

## 2. What days and times is Matsuba open? Is it open on Mondays?
The restaurant's own site (matsuba.co.uk) publishes these hours:
- Monday: 12pm-3pm and 5pm-10pm, marked "to confirm".
- Tuesday to Friday: 12pm-3pm and 5pm-10pm. The 3pm lunch closing time is marked "to confirm".
- Saturday: 12pm-10pm.
- Sunday: closed.

The site says Monday opening and the lunch closing time are still being checked with the restaurant, so whether it opens on Mondays is not settled. Other listings disagree. Harden's says closed Monday and Sunday, open Tuesday to Saturday 12-2:30pm and 6-10:30pm. OpenTable's summaries say Tuesday to Saturday in one version and Monday to Saturday in another. Please phone 020 8605 3513 to confirm, especially for a Monday.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (Opening hours section); https://matsuba.co.uk/llms.txt; Harden's; Foodle; Wanderlog; Trip.com; OpenTable summaries.
**Confidence:** medium for Tuesday to Sunday, low for Monday.
**Conflicts:** Yes.
- The official site says Monday is open, but flags it "to confirm".
- Harden's says Monday is closed.
- OpenTable's two summaries contradict each other: Monday to Saturday in one, Tuesday to Saturday in the other.
- Foodle and Wanderlog list Monday to Friday 12-3pm and 5-10pm, which matches the official site.
- Harden's and Trip.com show 2:30pm lunch and 10:30pm closing, against the official 3pm and 10pm.
- OpenTable's alternative gives a Saturday lunch end of 5pm, against the official all-day 12pm-10pm.

I believed the restaurant's own site as the primary source, but treated Monday and the lunch end time as unconfirmed because the site itself flags them. Harden's data looks older.

## 3. How do I book a table, and are there any booking conditions?
Tables are booked online through Favourite Table, Matsuba's booking system, via the "Book a table" button on matsuba.co.uk. Web reservations are for tables of up to 12 guests; for larger parties, call 020 8605 3513. A credit card is required to secure the booking. No money is taken at booking, but the restaurant reserves the right to charge £10 per person if you give less than 24 hours' cancellation notice or don't turn up. The tasting menu needs a pre-order. Matsuba is not on the OpenTable booking network, so don't try to book there.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (Book your table section); https://matsuba.co.uk/llms.txt; https://matsuba.co.uk/menu.html; OpenTable summaries.
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none. Wanderlog suggests booking at least 3 days ahead, but that is a tip rather than a rule. Harden's has no booking information.

## 4. Can I order takeaway or collection from Matsuba, and is there any discount?
Yes. You can order online for collection through Toast and pick up from 10 Red Lion Street. The site advertises 20% off collection, every day, with the kids menu excluded. There is also a takeaway menu PDF. The evidence says nothing about delivery.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (Collect from the corner); https://matsuba.co.uk/llms.txt; https://matsuba.co.uk/menu.html; Wanderlog (lists takeout).
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none

## 5. What are Matsuba's signature or speciality dishes?
The restaurant's own site calls the Tuna Tataki (£14.9, torch-seared tuna with mooli and ponzu) "our signature appetizer". The Matsuba Roll is a large 8-piece special roll created exclusively by Matsuba, with prawn tempura, avocado, cucumber, spring onion, tobiko and lemon mayo (£12.9 for a half portion or £22.9). There is also a 14-dish tasting menu at £100 per person (minimum 2, pre-order, complimentary Prosecco), featuring a "Lion King" nigiri of salmon aburi with cheddar. Reviewers praise the sushi, and one calls the Geisha combo (£79.9, a 33-piece chef's selection) a good choice for nigiri lovers. Wanderlog also recommends the tuna tataki.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (menu case); https://matsuba.co.uk/menu.html; search summaries (reviews); Wanderlog; Harden's.
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** The tasting menu price differs. Harden's lists £65, 14 courses, while the official site says £100 per person (minimum 2). I believed the official site, which was updated in August 2026. Harden's data looks older.

## 6. Do they do aburi (flame-seared) sushi? Name some of the aburi dishes.
Yes. The menu has an Aburi Nigiri section: toro (fatty tuna) £11.9, shake (salmon) £7.9, hotate (scallop) £9.9, suzuki (seabass) £8.9, maguro (tuna) £9.9, hamachi (yellowtail) £9.9, saba (mackerel) £6.9 and unagi (eel) £8.9. The evidence doesn't say whether these prices are per piece or per order.

Other flame-seared dishes:
- Salmon Aburi Carpaccio (£13.9): torch-seared salmon with yuzu miso dressing.
- Tuna Tataki (£14.9): torched-seared tuna.
- Salmon Combo_B (£40.9): includes 4 aburi nigiri.
- Lion King nigiri: salmon aburi with cheddar, on the tasting menu.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/menu.html (Aburi Nigiri, appetizers, Salmon Combo_B and tasting menu entries); https://matsuba.co.uk/ (tasting menu teaser).
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none. Third-party pages don't mention aburi at Matsuba, but they are less detailed than the official menu.

## 7. How much is the Tuna Tataki?

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/menu.html; https://matsuba.co.uk/ (lit menu case).
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none. Harden's gives only generic starter, main and pudding prices (£13.90, £20.40, £6.90), which are older and not item-specific.

## 8. Do they have vegan or gluten-free options?

Items marked "v" are suitable for vegetarians. The site also lists a Vegan menu PDF. Gluten-free: the site lists a Gluten-free menu PDF, but marks it "TO CONFIRM", so I can't confirm it exists or what it covers. The evidence doesn't say whether the kitchen can cater for coeliacs. The site asks you to tell staff about any allergy or dietary requirement, so call or tell them when booking.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/menu.html (v/vg markings, PDF links, allergy note); https://matsuba.co.uk/llms.txt.

## 9. Is it independent / family-run, and how long has it been open?
It is family-run and first opened in 2004, per the restaurant's own site. Harden's adds that it is run by a Korean family, which fits the Korean dishes (bibimbap) on the menu. OpenTable and Wanderlog also say it has operated since 2004. The evidence doesn't explicitly say "independent", but nothing suggests a chain.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (hero text and footer); https://matsuba.co.uk/llms.txt; Harden's; OpenTable summaries; Wanderlog.
**Confidence:** high for family-run and 2004, medium for "independent".
**Conflicts:** none. Harden's "sterling service for 17 years" comes from an older edition and fits roughly with 2004 for that edition.

Matsuba, 10 Red Lion Street, is the best fit in these results. It serves aburi nigiri (toro, salmon, scallop, seabass, yellowtail and more), Salmon Aburi Carpaccio and torched Tuna Tataki. Reviewers praise its sushi and service, with ratings of about 4.4 to 4.6 (Google 4.4 from 261 reviews, OpenTable 4.5 from about 210). Wanderlog ranks it #1 among Japanese restaurants in Richmond-upon-Thames.

The caveat is tonight's availability. 26 October 2026 is a Monday, and Matsuba's Monday opening is unconfirmed. The official site marks Monday as "to confirm" and Harden's says closed. Phone 020 8605 3513 before setting out; booking is via Favourite Table, with a £10 per person charge for late cancellation or no-show.
