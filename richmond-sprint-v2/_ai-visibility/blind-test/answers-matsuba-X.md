# Answers: Matsuba, Richmond (evidence pack X)

## 1. Where exactly is Matsuba in Richmond, and what's its phone number?
Matsuba is at 10 Red Lion Street, Richmond, TW9 1RW, in or on the fringe of the town centre. Harden's places it near St Mary Magdalene. The phone number is 020 8605 3513. The official site also lists info@matsuba.co.uk.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/reservations/ (address, phone, email); https://www.hardens.com/az/restaurants/richmond/tw9/matsuba.htm; https://foodle.pro/restaurants/richmond/matsuba; https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1197161; Trip.com listing
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** Minor. Harden's lists a different email and website (matsuba-restaurant.com), but that domain does not resolve. I went with the official matsuba.co.uk details. The address and phone number agree across every source.

## 2. What days and times is Matsuba open? Is it open on Mondays?
According to the restaurant's own website, it is open Monday to Friday 12pm-3pm and 5pm-10pm, and Saturday 12pm-10pm. It is closed on Sunday, so Monday is a normal opening day. Third-party listings disagree (see below), so ring 020 8605 3513 to confirm before a special trip.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/reservations/ and the footer of the homepage and menu pages; Foodle and Wanderlog (both match the official hours); Harden's; OpenTable summaries; Trip.com
**Confidence:** medium
**Conflicts:** Yes, and they matter for Mondays.
- Official site, Foodle and Wanderlog: Mon-Fri 12-3 and 5-10, Sat 12-10, Sun closed.
- Harden's: Tue-Sat 12-2:30 and 6-10:30, closed Monday and Sunday, and its page says "Closed today".
- OpenTable summaries are inconsistent with each other (Mon-Sat lunch and dinner, or Tue-Sat with Sat lunch only to 5pm).
- Trip.com: 12:00-14:30 and 18:00-22:30.
I believed the official site because it is the restaurant's own current information and three sources match it. The third-party listings look older or are inconsistent with each other. The Monday point is still not fully settled, because Harden's explicitly says closed Mondays.

## 3. How do I book a table, and are there any booking conditions (deposits, no-show charges, group size limits)?
The official site's "Book a table" button links to an online booking page at https://booking.favouritetable.com/?SiteCode=2122. You can also phone 020 8605 3513 or email info@matsuba.co.uk. The reservations page lists no deposit, cancellation, no-show or dress-code policy, and no group size limit, so I can't confirm any of those. The homepage calls it "perfect for groups", but gives no numbers. The tasting menu needs pre-ordering and is for a minimum of 2 people. Wanderlog suggests booking at least 3 days ahead at peak times. OpenTable says Matsuba is not on its booking network, so don't try to book there.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/reservations/; https://matsuba.co.uk/ (homepage); https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/ (tasting menu); https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1197161; OpenTable search summaries
**Confidence:** medium (booking method high; conditions are simply not stated)
**Conflicts:** None on the booking method. Wanderlog shows its own date/time selector, but the official site's link goes to Favourite Table.

## 4. Can I order takeaway or collection from Matsuba, and is there any discount?
Yes. The homepage says "Takeout now available" and links to online ordering at https://order.toasttab.com/online/matsuba-10-red-lion-street. A takeaway menu PDF is also linked, though its text could not be read. I found no standing takeaway discount or offer. The homepage has no offers or promotional dates. The only promotion found was a free "innocent" kids' drink with any Kids Meal, which ran until 1 September 2026, so it has ended. There is also a "Lunch & Early Evening" promotional image, but its details could not be read. The evidence doesn't say whether it's collection, delivery or both.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (homepage and fetch notes); https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/ (Kids Menu, Lunch & Early Evening); takeaway PDFs (no extractable text)
**Confidence:** medium
**Conflicts:** none

## 5. What are Matsuba's signature or speciality dishes?
The official site names the Tuna Tataki (torch-seared tuna with mooli and ponzu) as its "signature appetizer". It also promotes its sushi and sashimi ("The best sushi in Richmond") and the large 8-piece Special Rolls (for example the Matsuba Roll, Spider Roll and Dynamite Roll). Other items on the menu are the Black Cod Saikyo-yaki and the Salmon Aburi Carpaccio. Wanderlog also recommends the tuna tataki, and reviewers mention sushi sets, stonepot dishes and the Geisha combo. The Geisha combo is a 33-piece chef's selection at £79.90.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ (homepage); https://matsuba.co.uk/culinary-history/; https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/; https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1197161; OpenTable and Harden's review snippets
**Confidence:** high for Tuna Tataki; medium for the rest (they are highlights rather than officially labelled specialities)
**Conflicts:** none

## 6. Do they do aburi (flame-seared) sushi? Name some of the aburi dishes.
Yes. The menu has a full Aburi Nigiri section: toro (fatty tuna) £11.90, maguro (tuna) £9.90, hamachi (yellowtail) £9.90, hotate (scallop) £9.90, suzuki (seabass) £8.90, unagi (eel) £8.90, shake (salmon) £7.90 and saba (mackerel) £6.90. Other torched dishes are the Salmon Aburi Carpaccio (£13.90) and the Tuna Tataki (£14.90). The Salmon Combo_B (£40.90) includes 4 pieces of aburi nigiri. The tasting menu has a "Lion King" salmon aburi nigiri with cheddar.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/ (Sushi & Sashimi, Appetizers, Tasting Menu)
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none

## 7. How much is the Tuna Tataki?
The official menu lists Tuna Tataki at £14.90. It is a starter of torch-seared tuna slices with mooli and ponzu sauce. Prices include VAT, and a discretionary 12.5% service charge is added to the bill.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/ (Appetizers, Notices)
**Confidence:** high (the menu page may be updated at any time)
**Conflicts:** none. No other source gives this dish's price.

## 8. Do they have vegan or gluten-free options?
Yes, probably, but I couldn't read the dedicated menus. The menu page links to a vegan menu and a gluten-free menu as PDFs, and the vegan PDF had no extractable text. The main menu also marks several dishes vegan (vg) or vegetarian (v). **Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/ (Lunch Menu markings, links, Notices); vegan PDF (no text); gluten-free PDF (not fetched)
**Confidence:** medium
**Conflicts:** none

## 9. Is it independent / family-run, and how long has it been open?
It describes itself as family-run (the "Our Story" page), and Harden's says it is run by a Korean family. Harden's review quote calls it a "Lovely, tiny Japanese café run by a friendly family". The restaurant first opened in 2004, according to the official site and OpenTable. The evidence doesn't use the word "independent", but I found nothing suggesting it is part of a chain.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/ and https://matsuba.co.uk/culinary-history/; https://www.hardens.com/az/restaurants/richmond/tw9/matsuba.htm; OpenTable summaries; Wanderlog
**Confidence:** high for family-run and 2004; medium for "independent" (inferred)
**Conflicts:** Minor. Harden's "17 years" of service comes from an older edition, and the 2004 opening date is the more reliable figure. Wanderlog also says "operating since 2004".

## 10. I'm in Richmond tonight with a friend who loves flame-seared sushi. Where would you recommend, and why? How confident are you?
I'd recommend Matsuba, at 10 Red Lion Street. It is the only place in my results with confirmed flame-seared dishes on its menu. It serves eight types of aburi nigiri (including toro, scallop, salmon and yellowtail), plus Salmon Aburi Carpaccio and Tuna Tataki. It is also well rated: OpenTable gives about 4.5 from roughly 210 ratings, Google 4.4, and Wanderlog ranks it #1 of Richmond's Japanese restaurants. Phone 020 8605 3513 first to check it is open and has space. The sources disagree on opening days, and Harden's says closed Mondays while the official site says open. Book via the website if you can. Sticks'n'Sushi (80 George Street) is the other highly rated option, but the evidence only describes it as sushi and yakitori skewers, with no mention of aburi.

**Sources:** https://matsuba.co.uk/matsuba-menu/; https://matsuba.co.uk/reservations/; https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1197161; OpenTable search summaries (Query 1, 2, 3); Harden's
**Confidence:** medium. High that Matsuba fits "flame-seared sushi". Medium-low that it is open and bookable tonight, because the hours conflict and I can't see live availability.
**Conflicts:** Yes, on opening days (see Q2). I believed the official site (open Monday to Saturday). Ratings also differ slightly between sources (OpenTable 4.5/4.6, Google 4.4, Tripadvisor 4.0, Trip.com 4.4 and 5), so treat them as approximate.
