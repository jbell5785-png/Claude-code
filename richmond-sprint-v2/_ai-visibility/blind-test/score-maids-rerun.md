## Verdict

Set Q serves visitors clearly better: 18/20 with no false statements, against P's 10/20 with two. Q's main advantage is the actual tearoom menu. It gives "High Tea: £24.50… two scones with clotted cream (or butter) and jam, and your choice from the patisserie or a Maids of Honour tart", the £3.50 tearoom tart price and the full Prosecco and Champagne ladders, all matching the 2024 menu. P could only say "I could not find in-shop prices" and fell back on Deliveroo prices for two. P's worst error, made in both Q4 and Q10, is telling afternoon-tea guests that "between 12:00 and 15:00, bookings and walk-ins have a minimum spend of £20 per person". That is the Luncheon rule, and it could put people off a midday tea. Q is not flawless. It invents a provenance claim ("the official site itself flags that date 'TO CONFIRM'") and files the Council's "still owns and runs" under documented history without noting that the business says otherwise. P handled both of those history points more carefully.
