# Answers: Tide Tables Café, Richmond

## 1. Where exactly is Tide Tables café?
Tide Tables Café is in a converted arch beneath Richmond Bridge, in Richmond, south-west London. The address is 2 The Arches, Richmond, Surrey, TW9 1TH. Thatsup says the nearest Underground station is Richmond, about 700m away. The café also has a large garden with three plane trees, right by the Thames. The official site says it is just by the embarkation point for boats to Hampton Court and to the city.

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Our location"; Contact page); thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; talesfromthechair/roambyhelenray substack "sip stroll 459"; thamespath.org.uk 2017 article.
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none on the address. The Contact page writes "London TW9 1TH" and the Home page writes "Richmond / Surrey". These are the same place.

## 2. What are its opening hours?
I can't give you confirmed hours. The best current source is the Thatsup listing, last updated 6 June 2026. It shows 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM every day of the week. The official site only says both cafés are "open during daylight hours". It has no hours on its Contact page, and the page is marked "Copyright 2018", so it may be out of date. A 2017 Thames Path piece says only that it is "open all day". Check the café's phone or social media before you go. The official site also warns it gets very crowded on sunny days.

**Sources:** thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Our location"; Contact); thamespath.org.uk/2017/11/27/tide-times-cafe-under-richmond-bridge/.
**Confidence:** low to medium
**Conflicts:** "Daylight hours" (official, so it varies by season) versus a fixed 7:30 to 18:00 (Thatsup) versus "all day" (Thames Path, no times). I put most weight on Thatsup because it is the only source with specific times and it is the most recently updated. The two other sources do not contradict it directly. A search for opening hours returned only unrelated tide-prediction pages. A search summary also said it found no hours.

## 3. What's the phone number and email?

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Our community"; Contact); thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/ and /hollyhock-cafe/.
**Confidence:** high for the phone number. Medium-high for the email, because the site was last updated around 2018.
**Conflicts:** none. The two phone numbers match once the formatting differences are ignored. Thatsup gives the website as tidetablescafe.co.uk, but that domain returned an error when fetched. The working official site is tidetablescafe.com.

## 4. What food does it serve? Name some dishes.
The food is vegetarian. The official site lists Popina tarts, vegetable lasagne, jacket potatoes and pies, and says the menu is a sample that is subject to change. Thatsup mentions salads, pastries, cakes and tarts, plus fresh juices and organic wines and beers. A 2017 Thames Path writer had a vegetarian lasagne and salad for £10, and said weekday coffee refills were free. A 2024 blog praises the "frothy cappuccino and vegetarian treats". One Airbnb local tip says it is "not known for its food", but that coffee, tea and soft drinks do just fine. I couldn't find a current menu or prices: the official site's "view our menu here" has no working link.

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Supporting Fairtrade" and "Menu"); thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; thamespath.org.uk 2017 article; substack "sip stroll 459"; airbnb.cz/things-to-do/places/303427.
**Confidence:** medium
**Conflicts:** Opinions differ on how good the food is. The Thames Path writer says "good food", while one Airbnb tipster says it is not known for its food. I report both. The £10 price is from 2017.

## 5. Is the food organic / Fairtrade, and are there vegetarian or vegan options?
Yes to all three. The official site says that many products are organic or Fairtrade. It lists herbal teas, Fairtrade gourmet coffee, freshly squeezed organic juices and smoothies, organic milk, and wine and beer. It says "the food is all vegetarian, and there are vegan and gluten-free dishes, too". Thatsup describes the menu as "fully vegetarian and vegan". Country & Town House says the sister café Hollyhock sources "100 percent fair-trade ingredients".

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Organic products" and "Supporting Fairtrade"); thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; countryandtownhouse.com (Hollyhock entry).
**Confidence:** high for vegetarian, medium for vegan and gluten-free. These claims come from the café's own page, which dates from about 2018.
**Conflicts:** A search summary and Thatsup call the menu "entirely vegetarian and vegan". The official site is more precise: all vegetarian, with some vegan and gluten-free dishes. I believed the official site. Don't assume every item is vegan. The "100 percent fair-trade" claim is about Hollyhock, so I did not apply it to Tide Tables.

## 6. Can I book a table or a party there? How?
I found no table booking system. Country & Town House says "walk-ins only" for the café pair, and the official Contact page has no reservation or booking information. The mobile number comes from a page that appears to date from around 2018, so confirm it by phoning the café. At Hollyhock you can bring your own alcohol for a party, according to Thatsup.

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Our community"; Contact); countryandtownhouse.com (Hollyhock entry); thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/hollyhock-cafe/.
**Confidence:** medium

## 7. Is there outdoor seating?
Yes. The café has a large riverside garden with three plane trees, and the official site says the Thames views are some of the best in London. A Thames Path writer says you can sit outside on the downstream side, and that blankets can be borrowed from the basket at the door. Thatsup also lists outdoor seating. The official site says people are out in the garden all year round and that it gets very crowded on sunny days.

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Contact; Home, "Our location"); thamespath.org.uk 2017 article; discoveryrichmond.com/?p=3713; thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; substack "sip stroll 459".
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none

## 8. Is it connected to any other café nearby, and how do I walk there?
Yes. Hollyhock Café is its sister café, and both share the same menu (per Thames Path). Hollyhock is in Terrace Gardens, at 146 Petersham Rd, Richmond TW10 6UX, with a view over the gardens and the Thames. For the walk, the Thames Path article says you go upstream (away from the bridge, along the towpath) and pass through a tunnel. A search summary says the same: Hollyhock is "reached by a tunnel from the towpath upstream of the bridge". I found no distance or walking time, and the Thames Path page text is garbled, so the directions are approximate.

**Sources:** tidetablescafe.com (Home, "Our location"); thamespath.org.uk 2017 article; thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/hollyhock-cafe/; countryandtownhouse.com; wanderlog.com Hollyhock listing.
**Confidence:** high that they are connected, medium on the walking route
**Conflicts:** none on the connection. The Hollyhock postcode (TW10 6UX) is consistent across the official site, Thatsup and Country & Town House. The Thames Path says "share the same menu as Tide Times", which I took as a typo for Tide Tables.

## 10. A relaxed riverside café in Richmond for a lazy Sunday morning coffee and something to eat?
I'd suggest Tide Tables Café. It sits in an arch under Richmond Bridge, with a large riverside garden and views of the Thames. Reviewers describe it as a spot to "watch the world and the river go by", with good coffee and a warm, relaxed atmosphere. The food is vegetarian, with some vegan and gluten-free options. Thatsup lists 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM every day, which would cover a Sunday morning. Arrive early, as the official site says it gets crowded on sunny days. If you want a quieter option, Hollyhock Café is the sister café in Terrace Gardens. It is listed at 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM daily and has a better rating (Google 4.6 versus 4.3), but it is further from the river itself. It is higher up and looks over the Thames. Both only suit you if you are happy with a vegetarian menu. I'm fairly confident about the recommendation, but I could not verify current hours, menu or prices, and most written reviews are several years old.

**Sources:** thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/ and /hollyhock-cafe/; tidetablescafe.com; thamespath.org.uk 2017 article; discoveryrichmond.com/?p=3713; airbnb.cz/things-to-do/places/303427; substack "sip stroll 459"; search results for "riverside cafe Richmond Thames" and "vegetarian cafe Richmond riverside".
**Confidence:** medium. High that it is a good match, but low on the exact current hours.
**Conflicts:** Opinions on the food differ (see Q4): one tipster says it is not known for its food, while a Thames Path writer praised it. Hours also vary between sources (see Q2). I leaned on the café's own site and the most recent listing. I left out other places the search returned, such as Gaucho, Scott's and Bill's, because the evidence describes them as upmarket restaurants rather than relaxed coffee spots. I also ignored places that are not in Richmond, London (Reading, Richmond VA, and the Bradenton, Florida "Tide Tables Restaurant and Marina").
