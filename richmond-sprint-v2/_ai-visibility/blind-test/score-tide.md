## Verdict

Set Y serves visitors better. Both sets are free of outright invention, but Y consistently follows the café's own rules where the facts are uncertain. On opening hours it leads with "Open during daylight hours" and the phone number, and it treats third-party hours as "a rough guide only". It names Grotto Gate on the walk to Hollyhock, and on dogs it says plainly "Not confirmed by the café". Set X is accurate on the stable facts but leans on third-party sources where the café is silent. It "put most weight on Thatsup" for hours, tells a Sunday visitor that 7:30–18:00 "would cover a Sunday morning" (other listings disagree, and one shows Sunday closed), and answers the dog question with "Probably". Y's small lapses are a Hollyhock sausage roll presented as a Tide Tables dish and "the café asks you to phone" for bookings, and neither misleads a visitor.
