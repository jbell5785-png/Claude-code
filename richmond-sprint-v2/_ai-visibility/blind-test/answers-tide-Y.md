# Answers: Tide Tables Café, Richmond (evidence pack Y)

## 1. Where exactly is Tide Tables café?
Tide Tables Café is at 2 The Arches, Richmond, Surrey TW9 1TH. It is in a converted arch beneath Richmond Bridge, right on the Thames Path. The café says that from Richmond you should head for Richmond Bridge, a few minutes away, and it is in the arch beneath. Thatsup.co.uk puts the nearest Underground station (Richmond) about 700m away.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (Find us section, JSON-LD); https://tidetablescafe.com/llms.txt; https://thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none. The official site, the blog and the listings all give the same address. (The "Tide Tables Restaurant and Marina" in Bradenton, Florida is a different business and was ignored.)

## 2. What are its opening hours?
The café's own site says only "Open during daylight hours" and tells you to phone +44 (0)20 8948 8285 for specifics. It does not publish day-by-day times. A third-party listing (thatsup.co.uk, updated 6 June 2026) shows 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM every day, but that may not match the café's daylight-hours approach, which would vary by season. A 2017 Thames Path article said it is "open all day". I'd treat 7:30 to 6:00 as a rough guide only and ring ahead, especially in winter.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (Opening hours); https://tidetablescafe.com/llms.txt; https://thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; https://www.thamespath.org.uk/2017/11/27/tide-times-cafe-under-richmond-bridge/
**Confidence:** medium (the official source gives no fixed times)
**Conflicts:** The official site (daylight hours, no fixed times) conflicts loosely with thatsup (fixed 7:30 to 6:00). I believed the official site for the rule and offered thatsup only as an indicative range, because it is third-party and the café itself says day-by-day times are not published. One search run on "opening hours" returned only unrelated tide-prediction pages, so it gave nothing.

## 3. What's the phone number and email?
Phone: +44 (0)20 8948 8285. Email: hey@tidetablescafe.com. The café says email is only monitored periodically, so for anything urgent you should phone. For party enquiries it gives a separate mobile number, 07922 474327.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (Get in touch, JSON-LD); https://tidetablescafe.com/llms.txt
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none. Thatsup writes the phone number as 020-894 882 85, which is the same number. Thatsup and Country & Town House list the website as tidetablescafe.co.uk, but the official site is tidetablescafe.com. The .co.uk address returned an error (HTTP 503) when fetched, so I'd use the .com address.

## 4. What food does it serve? Name some dishes.
The official site lists Popina tarts, vegetable lasagne, jacket potatoes, pies, vegetable and bean chilli with brown rice, and hearty salads. It also serves coffee, tea (a large range of herbal teas), fresh juices and smoothies, and organic wines and beers. Reviewers also mention frothy cappuccinos, vegetarian sausage rolls and cakes. The café does not publish a current menu or prices. The menu page is only a sample layout, so phone for the day's specials. One 2017 visitor paid £10 for lasagne and salad, but that is old, and one reviewer says it is better known for drinks than for food.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (On the board); https://tidetablescafe.com/menu.html; https://thamespath.org.uk article (2017); https://talesfromthechair.substack.com/p/sip-stroll-459; https://www.countryandtownhouse.com/food-and-drink/the-very-best-places-to-eat-in-richmond/ (the sausage roll comes from the Hollyhock entry); airbnb.cz tips
**Confidence:** medium-high (dishes are named on the official site, but there is no current menu or prices)
**Conflicts:** The "not known for its food" comment (an Airbnb tip from 2019) contrasts with "good food" (Thames Path, 2017) and "great food" (the café itself). I treated these as differing opinions and reported both. The vegetarian sausage roll was written about Hollyhock and the shared menu, not Tide Tables directly.

## 5. Is the food organic / Fairtrade, and are there vegetarian or vegan options?
Yes. All food is vegetarian, and there are vegan and gluten-free dishes too. The café says many of its products are organic or Fairtrade, all its milk is organic, its coffee is Fairtrade gourmet coffee, and its juices are freshly squeezed organic ones. It also serves organic wines and beers. It describes itself as "passionate about the environment and animal welfare". A line saying "all our wines are vegan" is marked "TO CONFIRM" on the site, so check if that matters to you.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (On the board); https://tidetablescafe.com/menu.html; https://tidetablescafe.com/llms.txt; https://thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/
**Confidence:** high for vegetarian, vegan and organic or Fairtrade; low for the claim that all wines are vegan
**Conflicts:** One search summary called the café "entirely vegetarian and vegan", while the official site says all vegetarian with vegan and gluten-free dishes. I believed the official wording, because not everything is vegan.

## 6. Can I book a table or a party there? How?
There is no online booking, and the café asks you to phone. For parties, the site gives a number, 07922 474327. It says the café can offer indoor and outdoor space and seating and can cater fully. The main number is +44 (0)20 8948 8285. Note that the site marks the booking and party-contact line "TO CONFIRM", so verify details by phone. Country & Town House says Hollyhock is walk-in only.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (Get in touch, Parties under the arch); https://tidetablescafe.com/llms.txt; https://www.countryandtownhouse.com/food-and-drink/the-very-best-places-to-eat-in-richmond/
**Confidence:** medium-high
**Conflicts:** none on the substance. There is a minor tension: the site says "Phone to arrange" for parties, while Country & Town House says "walk-ins only" for the venues generally. I took the official site for parties and treated walk-ins as the norm for ordinary visits.

## 7. Is there outdoor seating?
Yes. There is a large garden with three plane trees and views over the Thames, which the café calls some of the best in London. People sit out all year round, but it gets very crowded on sunny days. A 2017 article says you can sit outside on the downstream side and borrow blankets from a basket at the door. Thatsup also tags outdoor seating.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (Out the far door); https://www.thamespath.org.uk/2017/11/27/tide-times-cafe-under-richmond-bridge/; https://discoveryrichmond.com/?p=3713; https://thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/
**Confidence:** high
**Conflicts:** none

## 8. Is it connected to any other café nearby, and how do I walk there?
Yes, its sister café is Hollyhock, in Terrace Gardens. The café's directions are to follow the towpath upstream from Richmond Bridge to Grotto Gate, then take the tunnel and steps up into Terrace Gardens. The site marks that route "TO CONFIRM", so check the signs on the day. Hollyhock's address is given as 146 Petersham Rd, Richmond TW10 6UX (the café's own site lists it as Terrace Gardens, TW10 6UX). It is also said to share the same menu. A 2017 article calls it a good view over the river from a high position.

**Sources:** https://tidetablescafe.com/ (Looking for Hollyhock?); https://thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/hollyhock-cafe/; https://www.countryandtownhouse.com/food-and-drink/the-very-best-places-to-eat-in-richmond/; https://www.thamespath.org.uk/2017/11/27/tide-times-cafe-under-richmond-bridge/
**Confidence:** medium-high (the walking route is flagged "TO CONFIRM" on the official site)
**Conflicts:** Hollyhock's hours are listed as daylight hours on the official site and 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM on thatsup. I did not rely on either for the walking directions. Both sources agree on the postcode TW10 6UX.

## 9. Is it dog-friendly?
Not confirmed by the café. The official site does not mention dogs. The only source that says so is a thatsup.co.uk listing, which tags Tide Tables as dog-friendly. The Thames Path article and the other pages don't mention dogs. I'd phone before bringing a dog, especially for indoor seating. (Hollyhock is separately described as dog-friendly in two sources.)

**Sources:** https://thatsup.co.uk/london/cafe/tide-tables-cafe/; https://tidetablescafe.com/ (no mention)
**Confidence:** low-medium (a single third-party tag, with no confirmation from the café)
**Conflicts:** none, but only one source covers it. The Hollyhock dog-friendly claims should not be assumed to apply to Tide Tables.

## 10. I want a relaxed riverside café in Richmond for a lazy Sunday morning coffee and something to eat. Where would you recommend, and why? How confident are you?
I'd suggest Tide Tables Café. It sits in a converted arch under Richmond Bridge on the Thames Path, with a large garden of plane trees and Thames views. Its all-vegetarian menu (with vegan and gluten-free options) includes tarts, lasagne, jacket potatoes and salads, and it serves organic and Fairtrade coffee, and tea. The listing says "stay long", and the café says you can hang out all day for the price of a coffee. Its Google rating is 4.3 from 1,161 reviews. Caveats: it gets crowded on sunny days, and reviewers praise the coffee and setting more than the food. The café publishes no fixed opening times (just "daylight hours"), and I found no Sunday-specific hours, though thatsup lists 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM daily, so phone +44 (0)20 8948 8285 before you go. If it is full or you want a view from above the river, the sister café Hollyhock in Terrace Gardens (Google 4.6) is a good alternative. Gaucho and Scott's have river terraces but are more upmarket restaurants. Overall confidence is medium: I'm confident about the location and character, less so about Sunday opening and the current menu.
