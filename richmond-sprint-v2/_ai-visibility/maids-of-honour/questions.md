# Questions a diner might ask an AI assistant (The Original Maids of Honour, Kew)
1. Where is The Original Maids of Honour, and what's the phone number?
2. What are its opening hours?
3. What is a Maids of Honour tart, and how much does one cost there?
4. Can I book afternoon tea there? How?
5. What set teas do they offer, and what do they cost?
6. Can I get a cooked breakfast there on a Thursday?
7. Can I get Maids of Honour tarts posted to me, and how should I warm them up?
8. What's the history of the tart and the tearoom? (Be clear about what is documented vs legend.)
9. When was the business founded?
10. I'm visiting Kew Gardens on Saturday and want a traditional English afternoon tea nearby. Where would you recommend, and why? How confident are you?
