# UK website pricing for independent restaurants, cafes and tearooms

Research accessed 2026-10-26. Evidence types: **published price** (provider's own page), **survey or average** (aggregated data), **article estimate** (third-party guide or vendor blog). Prices are GBP unless stated. VAT noted where stated; "VAT not stated" otherwise.

## Gaps
- No London or SW London hospitality specialist with a published price was found, and no premium or award-winning UK studio publishes an illustration-led restaurant price. The premium band is inferred from Yellowball's range, Futureproofs' Custom route and agency-guide estimates.
- No official UK survey (FSB, Enterprise Nation, DSIT) on small-business website spend was found. All "average" figures come from vendor-run analyses.

## Summary of typical ranges

- **(a) Template or DIY:** Squarespace or Wix £9-£29 per month (about £100-£350 per year). Cheap UK fixed-fee template builds £299-£999 (published median £520). Hospitality-specific entry points are Podium Design from £400 and Steony from £700. Restaurant platforms such as Flipdish are £119-£139 per month, ex-VAT, and include ordering.
- **(b) Bespoke freelancer:** Day rates £180-£550 (London median about £513). Custom 5-10 page sites £1,000-£5,000. £2,500-£6,000 is the London bespoke band. Published restaurant packages with booking sit at £1,500-£2,000 (Podium).
- **(c) Bespoke agency or premium:** Small hospitality agencies £2,250-£8,000 (Flavr, B3, Futureproofs Custom £4,995). General London agencies £6,000-£25,000. High-end hospitality studios from £7,500, typically £25k+ (Yellowball). Illustration is extra.
- **Care plans:** £30-£50 per month is the usual entry (Website Helper £30, Flavr £36, Steony £45, Mapletree £47). Median about £50. £100+ per month covers heavier content edits and security work. Hosting alone £5-£30 per month. Ad-hoc edits about £30 per hour.
