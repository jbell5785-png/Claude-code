## Typical ranges (summary)

1. Audits: free lead-magnet checks to about £250-£2,000 one-off; most credible published UK figure is £1,500 (Apex AI). Small-venue audit could sensibly sit under £500, but no UK source publishes that for restaurants.
2. Retainers: UK GEO entry from about £450/mo (NeuralAdX), mainstream £550-£1,800/mo, small-business agency £1,200-£3,000/mo, enterprise £5,000-£10,000+. Many agencies are quote-only.
3. Tools: entry $29-$99/mo (about £22-£74 converted): Otterly $29, Peec about $95, Semrush $99, Ahrefs custom prompts $50, BrightLocal $31-$41. Profound has no self-serve entry price now. Entry tiers track only 15-50 prompts.
4. Schema: about £110-£450 one-off for a small site from freelancer/agency benchmarks (US, converted); marketplace gigs lower; UK evidence is thin.
5. Local SEO reporting: UK retainers £400-£1,200/mo with monthly reporting; AI tracking not standard in them.

## Cautions

- Google: "There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary"; "no special schema.org structured data that you need to add"; "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup"; "Indexing and serving aren't guaranteed". Structured data should match visible text. Last updated 2025-12-10. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features (accessed 2026-10-26).
- No one can guarantee citations. An agency can guarantee deliverables, reporting and access, not placement; OpenAI says advertisers cannot "shape, rank, or alter ChatGPT's responses"; Google advises avoiding anyone guaranteeing first place. https://eseospace.com/blog/geo-agency-guarantees-red-flags/ (published 2026-10-13; agency blog, secondary). John Mueller quote was NOT found in that page.
- Measurement is noisy: a competitor test found the top-named brand changed 28-44% of the time across reruns, so 25-50 tracked prompts are only directional. https://www.honeyb.ai/blog/semrush-ai-visibility-toolkit (2026-10-10, competitor blog).
- Apex AI itself says no placement guarantees, and first citation signals take 30-60 days, durable results 90-180 days.
- UK regulation: no ASA/CMA guidance specific to GEO or AI-ranking promises was found. General CAP Code rules apply: claims must not be materially misleading (3.1), not exaggerate (3.11, 3.12), and need documentary evidence (3.7); marketers are responsible and ignorance of the technology is no defence (1.8). Bird & Bird summary of ASA guidance on AI claims, 2024-10-04: https://mediawrites.twobirds.com/post/102jkyz/the-asas-guidance-on-ai-as-a-marketing-term. A freelancer should therefore avoid "guaranteed ChatGPT ranking" wording.
- Many sources are vendor or agency marketing; vendor pricing pages for Otterly, Peec and Profound could not be read, so re-check before quoting.
