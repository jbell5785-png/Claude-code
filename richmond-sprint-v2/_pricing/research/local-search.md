# UK local-search service pricing for small hospitality businesses

Researched 2026-10-26 (all "accessed 2026-10-26" unless a page date is given). Evidence types: published price / marketplace listing / survey or average / article estimate.

Caveats up front:
- No source gave restaurant, cafe or tearoom-specific GBP prices. Hospitality is mentioned only in passing. London-specific figures come from one commercial blog (Kolonell). Nearly all UK figures are agency self-published or article estimates.
- Software prices are USD list prices from the vendors' own US-facing pages. No UK GBP price was found for any tool. Conversions below use an assumed 1 USD = 0.75 GBP (my assumption, not a sourced rate) and are marked "converted".
- Several sources contradict each other or show inconsistent dates (noted in the table). Treat all figures as indicative.

Takeaways for an add-on freelancer: Google lets anyone claim and manage free, so the market pays for time and results, not access. A defensible add-on band is about £150-£400 one-off setup and £100-£350 per month for GBP management including review replies and posts. Both sit well below agency retainers. The per-tool software cost (about £15-£50/mo) is a floor for any "review reply" line item.
