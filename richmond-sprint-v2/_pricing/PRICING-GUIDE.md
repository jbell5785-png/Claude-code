# Pricing guide: Richmond restaurant websites and add-ons

Market research accessed 26 October 2026. UK prices, mostly excluding VAT. The sources, with URLs, dates and evidence type for every figure, are in `research/`. Figures from search snippets or converted from US dollars are marked there; treat them as approximate.

## What the market charges

### 1. The website
| Option | Typical UK price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DIY template (Squarespace, Wix) | £100–£350 a year | The owner builds it. Generic. |
| Restaurant platforms (Flipdish, Owner.com and similar) | Flipdish £119–£139 a month ex-VAT | Includes online ordering. Template look. |
| Fixed-fee template build | £299–£999 (published median £520) | Hospitality entry points from £400 to £700. |
| **Bespoke freelancer** | **£1,000–£5,000; London £2,500–£6,000** | Day rates £180–£550 (London about £513). |
| Small hospitality agency | £2,250–£8,000 | e.g. Flavr from £2,250; Futureproofs Custom from £4,995. |
| London agency / premium studio | £6,000–£25,000+ | Illustration usually charged extra. |
| Care plan (hosting, updates) | £30–£50 a month | Ad-hoc edits about £30 an hour. |

No one publishes a price for an *illustration-led* restaurant site. That's the gap our concepts fill.

The Google profile itself is free. The client should stay the **owner** and add you as a **manager**: managers can't add or remove users.

### 3. AI visibility
| Service | Typical price |
|---|---|
| One-off AI visibility audit | Free (lead magnet) up to £250–£2,000; clearest UK published price £1,500 |
| Monthly AI-search retainer | UK from about £450; mainstream £550–£1,800 |
| AI tracking tools (your cost) | About £25–£75 a month at entry level; BrightLocal includes an AI tracker |
| Structured-data (schema) set-up | About £110–£450 one-off (US benchmarks, converted) |

**Caution:** Google says no special optimisation or structured data is needed to appear in AI Overviews, and that nothing is guaranteed. Sell accurate information and a monthly report, never a ranking.

## Before you quote
- **Confirm scope with each owner.** Matsuba and Maids already have booking systems and an online shop that stay as they are. Tide Tables has no menu, so writing it up is extra content work.
- **Your own business admin.** Check whether VAT applies to you (the current registration threshold is £90,000 turnover). Take a deposit, for example 50% upfront. Use a simple contract that covers ownership of the illustrations and the domain.
- **Prices drift.** Re-check a few competitors before each pitch.
