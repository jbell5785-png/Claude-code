The restaurant

A family-run Japanese restaurant on Red Lion Street, Richmond. First opened in 2004.

Matsuba’s own description is “sushi, sashimi and fine Japanese dishes with a modern twist”. The signature appetizer is Tuna Tataki. The large Special Rolls are made only here. At lunch there’s bento, and for an occasion, a tasting menu that starts with a glass of Prosecco.

The room is small and warm: dark wood slats, Edison bulbs, sake bottles on the shelf, brick and booths. Book a table, order for collection, or start with the menu.

We look forward to welcoming you to MATSUBA!
