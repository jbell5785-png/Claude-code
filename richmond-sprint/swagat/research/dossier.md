# Swagat Indian Cuisine: research dossier

86 Hill Rise, Richmond TW10 6UB · official site https://swagatcuisine.uk · all sources accessed 25 October 2026 unless stated.
Status key: **VERIFIED** = official site, or several agreeing sources. **REPORTED** = one third-party source (dated). **UNKNOWN** = not established.

Evidence (not for the repo): page text, screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844, and JSON audit data are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Claude-code/e50707d5-9ec5-571e-9915-9e7145ebe50f/scratchpad/prospects/swagat/pages/`.

## Summary

1. The business is an independent Indian restaurant at 86 Hill Rise, on Richmond Hill. It was founded in 2006 (Hayler, 2013) or 2007 (Travel Gourmet, 2016), and it calls itself "contemporary" or "modern" Indian.
2. The menu was captured in full and word for word, but the Menu page publishes it twice. One copy is page text with chilli icons; the other is the live online-ordering widget. The two disagree on at least 7 prices, several allergen codes and a few dishes.
3. Booking and ordering both run through the GloriaFood/foodbooking.com widget, which opens as an overlay. OpenTable is only linked from a review badge. Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat are named on the site but not linked.
4. Biggest website problems: the About Us page shows lorem ipsum and "Name" team placeholders. The Blogs page is empty. The Order Takeaway page has no way to order. On the Menu page, raw JSON appears as visible text, the content has no side margin, and on mobile the header logo overlaps the title. No phone number or email address is a tappable link.
5. Best brand hooks: the deep-maroon Devanagari-style wordmark, a strong vegetarian section (15 vegetarian mains, each also sold as a side), Kerala, Chettinad and Parsi dishes alongside north Indian classics, and new professional food photography on white marble. A river view is **not** reported by any source.

## Identity and positioning

- **Name as used:** "Swagat Indian Cuisine" (logo, copyright, hero). The site also uses "Swagat Cuisine", "Swagat Indian Restaurant" and "Swagat Restaurant". VERIFIED.
- **Self-description (site):** "Modern Indian Restaurant In Richmond Upon Thames" (H1); "a contemporary Indian Restaurant in Richmond upon Thames"; "Contemporary Indian Cuisine" (H2). VERIFIED.
- **Founders:** "established in 2006 by the wonderfully named Sudden Alberts and his business partner Krishnapal Negi" (Andy Hayler, 29 Jul 2013). REPORTED. The 2017 review reports "a partial change of ownership" and that the co-founder had moved to Atithi (Hayler, 30 May 2017; second part paraphrased). REPORTED. Harden's (2022 review) names Sudden Alberts as founder, "who also runs Atithi in Twickenham". REPORTED. **Staff names must not be used by designers.**
- **Chef named on homepage:** "Led by our esteemed chef – Sudden Alberts –". Third-party sources call this person the founder or business partner. Hayler 2013 and Travel Gourmet 2016 name other people as chef, and Hayler 2017 names a new head chef. Recorded as a conflict; do not use.
- **Cooking style:** Hayler (2013): "the usual north Indian staples, with a few less common dishes such as a curry involving sea bass". REPORTED. The menu text itself names "Traditional Kerala speciality" (Malabar Fish Curry), "A south Indian speciality" (Murgh Chetinaad), "Originating among the Parsi Zoroastrian Community" (Dhansak) and "A north Indian speciality" (Mutter Paneer). VERIFIED.
- **Positioning in press:** "refined take on traditional Indian cuisine, allied to a cosy but classy dining environment"; "vegetarian and vegan options are plentiful" (Sotheby's Realty journal, 7 Jan 2025). REPORTED. "A real boon in Richmond"; "hidden gem" (Harden's, 2022). REPORTED. "a touch above standard local Indian fare" (Travel Gourmet, 6 Apr 2016). REPORTED.
- **Room:** Hayler (2013): "small, quite cramped dining room and very small tables". Travel Gourmet (2016): "simple, less stylish than Tangawizi". REPORTED and dated; the room may have changed since. The About Us photos show a dark-wood interior and a burgundy shopfront with a white illuminated fascia.
- **Setting:** "on Richmond Hill, a short walk up from the cinema" (Hayler 2013); "a little way up Richmond Hill" (Travel Gourmet 2016). REPORTED. **View to the river: UNKNOWN.** No source describes a view from the restaurant. Travel Gourmet mentions the view from the top of the hill, before the meal.
- **Michelin:** "All three restaurants are recommended by Michelin" (Travel Gourmet, 2016). REPORTED; there is no current evidence, so do not use.

## Booking, takeaway and contact

- **Book:** Every "Book Table" / "Table Reservations" button (hero, mid-page, footer) opens the foodbooking.com reservation overlay. The form asks for number of people, date, time and optional comments, then "Next" (we did not go beyond "Next" and submitted nothing). The header "Book Table" goes to `https://swagatcuisine.uk/#book-table`, the "Reserve Your Table" section on the homepage. The page copy reads "Reserve your table using the below form. Alternatively, you can reserve by giving us a call on the following number: 020 8940 7557". OpenTable is reachable only through the OpenTable review badge image.
- **Order:** The hero "Order Takeaway" button opens the foodbooking.com ordering overlay (at about 06:00 UTC it read "We're closed now but you can pre-order for later."). The **nav** "Order Takeaway" link goes to /order-takeaway/, which has no order button, no widget and no platform links.
- **Contact form** (/contact/): Name, Email, Phone Number, "Email Email *" (a duplicated label), "How can we help you?" (General), Message, then "SEND MESSAGE". It has an embedded Google Map.
- **Allergen statements (verbatim):**
  - /menu/: "If you have any food allergies please inform a member of staff as some food items contain: Dairy (D), Eggs (E) Nuts (N) Gluten (G), Crustaceans (C), Mustard (M) Soya (S) and some are Vegan suitable (V)"
  - /order-takeaway/: "If you have any food allergies, please let us know by adding it into the order notes. Some of our food items contain:" / "Dairy (D), Eggs (E) Nuts (N) Gluten (G), Crustaceans (C), Mustard (M) and some are Vegan suitable (V)." (Soya is omitted.)
  - The key has no codes for fish, peanuts, sesame, celery, molluscs, lupin or sulphites, although fish dishes are listed. Note: "(V)" is defined as **Vegan suitable**, not vegetarian.

## Current website audit

Measured in Playwright headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844 (mobile emulation, DPR 2) on 25 Oct 2026. All 8 nav pages returned HTTP 200 in about 2.5–4.9 s to network idle. The only failed request was Google Analytics collection (aborted; not user-facing). No broken images were found.

**Strengths to keep**
- No horizontal overflow on any page at 390 px (scrollWidth = 390 on all 8).
- Homepage first screen on mobile shows the brand, the H1 and all three actions (VIEW MENU, Book Table, Order Takeaway) above the fold.
- Online booking and ordering are built in: one tap from the homepage hero opens a working reservation form or ordering menu.
- Allergen codes on every dish, plus main/side pricing for vegetarian dishes.
- The May 2024 marble-background photo set is consistent and appetising.
- The maroon accent (#7D1114) passes contrast at 10.7:1 on white.
- Third-party proof is shown up front: OpenTable, Google and Tripadvisor badges.

**Opportunities (with evidence)**
1. **Placeholder content is live.** About Us shows lorem ipsum under "Our Specialties" and "Meet The Team" with three "Name" cards that use the logo as the photo.
2. **Empty page in the nav.** /blogs/ has no content, no H1 and no meta description. Its footer differs from every other page ("Powered by Astra WordPress Theme").
3. **Order Takeaway is a dead end.** /order-takeaway/ has no order button, no widget and no links. Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat are plain text ("Uber eats, Just eats"). Ordering only works from the homepage hero button.
4. **Menu page breaks.**
   - Raw widget JSON is printed as visible text (`{"opening":"Opening Hours","pickup":"Pickup Service",…`), seen in the 1440 screenshot. No request failed, so real users probably see it too ([TO CONFIRM] on a real phone).
   - Content runs to the very left edge with 0 px gutter at both widths.
   - On mobile, the header logo box overlaps the "Menu" title.
   - "Menu" appears twice as H1.
   - The menu is published twice with conflicting prices (see Menu).
   - Mobile page height is 21,935 px.
   - Prices are aligned with typed spaces, so they wrap unevenly at 390 px.
5. **Invisible mobile nav on the Menu page.** The top-right header region at 390 px is solid white (25,200 of 25,200 sampled pixels are #FFFFFF). The white hamburger icon is only visible over hero images.
6. **Hours are hard to find.** They appear only in the footer: about 15,500 px down a 15,995 px homepage on mobile (social icons at 15,023 px, footer Book table at 15,787 px). Five sources give five different sets of hours. There is no Restaurant/LocalBusiness schema (the JSON-LD is only WebPage, WebSite and BreadcrumbList).
7. **Button styles are inconsistent.** In the hero, "VIEW MENU" is uppercase Roboto Slab 600 with 2 px letter-spacing and no underline, while "Book Table" and "Order Takeaway" are sentence case, weight 400 and underlined. The header "Book Table" is a filled maroon Arial-bold button. "SEND MESSAGE" is 22 px with 0 radius and 26 px tall. Labels switch between "Book Table", "Book table", "Table Reservations" and "VIEW ALL MENU".
8. **Tap targets are small.** At 390 px the hero buttons are 39 px tall; footer links are 24 px tall; Facebook and Instagram icons are 18×18 px. On the homepage 20 of 24 tap targets are under 44 px (some are full-width links that are only short). The open mobile nav items are 45 px tall, which is fine.
9. **Phone and email cannot be tapped.** No `tel:` or `mailto:` link exists on any page.
10. **Body text contrast.** #7A7A7A on white is 4.29:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 threshold for body text.
11. **Titles, metadata and copy slips.**
    - Titles mix "Swagat Cuisine - …" (home) with "… - Swagat Indian Cuisine".
    - The testimonial title reads "Testimonial s - Swagat Indian Cuisine".
    - The rating label reads "COSTUMER'S RATING".
    - The contact form label reads "Email Email *".
    - Gallery and home images mostly lack alt text (41 of 45 and 35 of 42 have empty alt).
    - Meta descriptions exist on 7 of 8 pages and are reasonable.
12. **The logo is tiny and blurry.** It is a 120×76 raster, shown at about 80 px wide in the header.
13. **The rating badges may be out of date.** They are static images uploaded 2024/02 (from the file path): "5/5 - 149 reviews" (OpenTable), "4.0/5 - 211 Google reviews", and a Tripadvisor badge with 4 of 5 bubbles. The Testimonials page shows "4.3/5" with no source.
14. **Gallery captions name dishes not on the menu** (see Menu).

**Taps to act (mobile, from the homepage):** Book takes 1 tap to open the overlay, then 3 selects and "Next", then further steps not explored. Order takes 1 tap (hero) to the ordering menu. Through the nav, ordering is impossible: Order Takeaway leads to a page with no ordering. Hours take a scroll of about 15,500 px, or the nav has no hours page at all.

## Audience (inference, not verified)

- Richmond and Richmond Hill locals and regulars. The testimonials describe long-term family use, and Harden's calls it a "hidden gem".
- Weekend visitors to Richmond Hill, the park and the riverside.
- Groups and families celebrating: 8 is the maximum online party size.
- Vegetarians: a full vegetarian main section with side portions, and repeated reviewer mentions.
- A takeaway audience split between the restaurant's own commission-free ordering and the delivery apps.

- Whether the Tripadvisor domain swagatindiancuisine.co.uk is live. Our request was reset at the proxy, so this is inconclusive. Harden's lists www.swagatcuisine.co.uk, which did not resolve from our environment.

- Interior today: the reviews describing a cramped room date from 2013 to 2016.
- Whether a vector logo or brand guidelines exist.
