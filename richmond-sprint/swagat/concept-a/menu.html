V means vegan suitable, not vegetarian. Vegetarian dishes are the ones under the Vegetarian headings. Marked V on our menu: Mix Veg Jalfreezie, Vegetable Biryani, Peas Pulao, Pulao rice, Mushroom Pulao, Jeera Rice, Steam rice, Tandoori Roti.

“If you have any food allergies please inform a member of staff as some food items contain: Dairy (D), Eggs (E) Nuts (N) Gluten (G), Crustaceans (C), Mustard (M) Soya (S) and some are Vegan suitable (V)”

Codes are shown as printed on our menu. Many dishes carry no code; that doesn’t mean they’re free from allergens, so please ask us. Chilli marks are shown as printed too.
