Fifteen vegetarian mains

Every one also comes as a side

Our vegetarian mains are a menu of their own. And as the menu says, “These dishes can also be ordered as a side.”

So a table can share four or five dishes instead of two. Put a few side portions together, add rice and bread, and you have a thali of your own.

V

V means vegan suitable. It isn’t the vegetarian mark: everything in this section is vegetarian, and only some dishes carry a V.
