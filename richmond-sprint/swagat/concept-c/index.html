Where it’s from

Four notes on the menu say where a dish comes from. Here they are, word for word, as the restaurant prints them.

  • Kerala

    Malabar Fish Curry

    “Traditional Kerala speciality cooked with coconut and tempered with mustard & curry leaves.”

    £15.00

  • South India

    Murgh Chetinaad

    “A south Indian speciality of chicken in medium spicy coconut sauce.”

    £14.00

  • Parsi

    Chicken Dhansak and Lamb Dhansak

    “Originating among the Parsi Zoroastrian Community.”

    Chicken £14.50 · lamb £17.99

  • North India

    Mutter Paneer

    “A north Indian speciality made with Peas and Cottage cheese”

    Main £9.75 · side £6.50

Malabar Fish Curry, Murgh Chetinaad and both Dhansaks sit with the non-vegetarian mains, served in 500 ml. Mutter Paneer is a vegetarian main that also comes as a side.
