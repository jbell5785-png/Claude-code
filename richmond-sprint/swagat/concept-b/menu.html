Which menu this is

Dishes, prices and codes follow the menu printed on the restaurant's menu page. Desserts and lassi appear only on its online ordering menu, so they're labelled.

Allergen key

  • DDairy
  • EEggs
  • NNuts
  • GGluten
  • CCrustaceans

  • SSoya

The restaurant's statement: If you have any food allergies please inform a member of staff as some food items contain: Dairy (D), Eggs (E) Nuts (N) Gluten (G), Crustaceans (C), Mustard (M) Soya (S) and some are Vegan suitable (V)

Chilli icons are shown as printed on the restaurant's menu, which gives no key for them.

The vegetarian view shows only the sections the menu itself heads “Vegetarian”: vegetarian starters and vegetarian mains. Rice, breads, raita, desserts and lassi aren't labelled either way, so they're hidden in this view; please ask the restaurant.

On this menu V means vegan suitable. It does not mean vegetarian.

Vegetarian dishes are listed under each “Vegetarian” heading. On this menu V means vegan suitable, not vegetarian.
