From the menu, in its own words

A menu that says where its dishes come from

Read the descriptions on Swagat's menu and the dishes start naming their origins. A fish curry from Kerala. Chicken from the south. Lentils from the Parsi community. Peas and paneer from the north. And one dish the kitchen keeps as its own.

Malabar Fish Curry

“Traditional Kerala speciality cooked with coconut and tempered with mustard & curry leaves.”

Main course, non-vegetarian

Kerala

The menu calls it a “Traditional Kerala speciality”.

Murgh Chetinaad

“A south Indian speciality of chicken in medium spicy coconut sauce.”

Main course, non-vegetarian

South India

“A south Indian speciality”, says the menu.

Dhansak

“Originating among the Parsi Zoroastrian Community.”

Made two ways on the menu: Chicken Dhansak, and Lamb Dhansak, “a combination of Lamb curry, lentils with a sweet, sour & spicy taste.”

The Parsi Zoroastrian community

Both Dhansak descriptions on the menu name the Parsi community.

Vegetarian

Fifteen vegetarian mains, and every one comes as a side

The vegetarian mains have their own section of the menu, and each one carries two prices: a main course size and a side dish size. Order a few as sides and they become a table to share.

“These dishes can also be ordered as a side. For each dish, there are two prices: the first for a main course size, and the latter for a side dish size.”

See the vegetarian menu

What (V) means here

On this menu V means vegan suitable. It doesn't mean vegetarian.

Dishes marked V: Mix Veg Jalfreezie, Vegetable Biryani, Peas Pulao, Pulao rice, Mushroom Pulao, Jeera Rice, Steam rice and Tandoori Roti.

The tandoor

From the clay oven

Six of the starters are grilled in the tandoor, by the menu's own account: King Prawn Naryali, Prawn Angare, Chicken Tikka, Tandoori Lamb Chops, Seekh Kebab and Paneer Tikka Shashlik. So is the Tandoori Chicken, served half or whole.

The breads come from it too: Tandoori Naan and Tandoori Roti, and a Chilly cheese Naan and Kheema Naan the menu describes as “baked in tandoor”.

Mix Grill Platter

To try several at once: “Combination of kebab includes prawn naryali, chick tikka, Seekh kebab, Lamb Chop and fish koliwada.” Priced per person.
