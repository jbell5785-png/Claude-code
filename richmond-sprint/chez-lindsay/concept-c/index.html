Buckwheat · served every day until 5pm

Les galettes

Our ‘galettes’ or buckwheat pancakes are a speciality. Cooked to order on a flat griddle, they are made from wonderful, nutty-tasting, organic buckwheat.

Galettes come with just about every savoury filling you can think of – perfect as a starter or main course.

Private dining · first floor

12–4012 to 40 guests, upstairs

Our light, first-floor private room is perfect for parties of 12 to 40 people.

Whether you are organising a business lunch or dinner, family celebration or just want to get together with friends, we will be happy to come up with the right food, the right wines, and the perfect decoration to suit your event and budget.

All our private dining functions are tailor-made (we don’t have set menus or a set formula) so do call us on 020 8948 7473 or email us now to discuss your requirements.
