Les galettes · every day until 5pm

Cooked to order on the griddle

Our galettes, buckwheat pancakes, are a speciality. Cooked to order on a flat griddle, they are made from wonderful, nutty-tasting, organic buckwheat, and come with just about every savoury filling you can think of: perfect as a starter or main course.

  • Complete Vegetarian£15

    With cheese, onion, mushrooms, spinach and tomato

A selection of our galettes is vegetarian and can be adapted to being vegan.

Bus services to Richmond are aplenty.
