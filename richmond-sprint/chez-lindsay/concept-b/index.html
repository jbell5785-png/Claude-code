Bienvenue

Bienvenue . . . and welcome to fresh, authentic French cooking from Brittany, right here on the banks of the Thames.

Having been here since 1988, we are very much part of the landscape on Hill Rise. As an independent restaurant, we would love to see you any time: for Sunday lunch, business brunch, film or theatre supper or a relaxed three-course dinner.

Les galettes

Our ‘galettes’ or buckwheat pancakes are a speciality. Cooked to order on a flat griddle, they are made from wonderful, nutty-tasting, organic buckwheat.

Galettes come with just about every savoury filling you can think of: perfect as a starter or main course.

The private room

12 to 40guests, upstairs, tailor-made

Our light, first-floor private room is perfect for parties of 12 to 40 people. Whether you are organising a business lunch or dinner, family celebration or just want to get together with friends, we will be happy to come up with the right food, the right wines, and the perfect decoration to suit your event and budget.

All our private dining functions are tailor-made (we don’t have set menus or a set formula), so do call us or email us to discuss your requirements.
