Studio review · Richmond, London · 25 October 2026

Three Richmond restaurants, nine website concepts

Each business was researched from public sources, given a written brief, and offered three genuinely different creative directions, each built as a working, responsive prototype with a full menu page.

  • 3 businesses
  • 9 concepts, each with a homepage and a menu
  • 0 console errors and 0 axe A/AA violations across all nine

Every concept is a speculative proposal, not an official website, and none was commissioned or endorsed by the business. Nothing has been published or sent. Facts the studio couldn't verify are marked "to confirm" on the pages.

Chez Lindsay

Breton restaurant and wine bar · 11 Hill Rise, by Richmond Bridge · since 1988

Why: its 2014 site has no mobile layout. At 390px it is a fixed 1000px page with the logo cut off, all six menus are PDFs, and the hours (closed Tuesdays, galettes and set lunch until 5pm, the carte from 5pm) are hard to piece together. The lease changed hands in 2024, so the new owners are a natural audience.

Matsuba

Family-run Japanese restaurant · 10 Red Lion Street · since 2004

Why: its site is a generic template, with one filler line repeated six times and "best sushi in London" on every page. On a phone, the first screen has no hours, menu or ordering link. Its printed menus already have a burgundy-and-gold brand that the website never uses.

Recommended

A · "Noren"

It brings their own print colours online, makes "small and family-run" the point, and still puts Book and Order (20% off collection) on the first screen of a phone.

QA passed on all three. The email draft also carries a separate, optional security courtesy note about a script on their current site.

Swagat

Indian restaurant · 86 Hill Rise, Richmond Hill

Why: its site is unfinished where customers will notice. About Us shows lorem ipsum, the Menu page shows raw ordering-system code, the mobile menu icon is white on white, and the hours sit about 15,500px down the phone homepage. Its real strengths, regional dishes and fifteen vegetarian mains, are buried.

How these were made: public research only, with sources and dates in each dossier. Each concept was designed and built by a separate builder working from the shared spec in _shared/tech-spec.md. Every concept was then checked in Chromium at 390, 768 and 1440px for console errors, failed requests, broken links, overflow, layout shift and axe-core WCAG A/AA, and its screenshots were reviewed by eye. Not tested: Safari or Firefox, real phones, or real screen readers.

To view the concepts, serve this folder (for example python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory richmond-sprint) and open http://localhost:8000. Markdown files open as plain text in a browser.
