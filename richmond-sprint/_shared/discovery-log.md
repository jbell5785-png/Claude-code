# Discovery log

Research date: 25 October 2026. Method: web search, public listings (Harden's, Time Out, OpenTable, CAMRA, The Infatuation), and loading official sites in a standard headless Chromium browser at 1440px and 390px. No access controls were bypassed; sites that refused automated requests were not forced.

## Shortlist and decisions

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chez Lindsay, 11 Hill Rise, TW10 6UQ | Breton crêperie, restaurant and wine bar by Richmond Bridge | chez-lindsay.co.uk | Footer "© Chez Lindsay 2014".
